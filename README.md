## 📌 Project Overview

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations because high employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect workforce stability.

This project analyzes employee data to identify attrition patterns, understand the major factors associated with employee turnover, identify high-risk workforce segments, and provide actionable HR retention recommendations.

The complete project uses:

- 🐍 **Python** for data cleaning, feature engineering and exploratory data analysis
- 🗄️ **SQL** for business analysis and KPI calculations
- 📊 **Power BI** for interactive dashboards and visualization
- 📐 **DAX** for HR metrics and analytical measures
- 🐙 **GitHub** for project documentation and portfolio presentation

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall employee attrition
- Identify high-attrition departments
- Identify high-risk job roles
- Analyze the impact of overtime
- Analyze employee job satisfaction
- Analyze environment satisfaction
- Analyze work-life balance
- Analyze business travel patterns
- Analyze employee income
- Analyze employee tenure
- Analyze promotion gaps
- Analyze training frequency
- Analyze job involvement
- Identify potential employee retention risks
- Provide actionable HR recommendations
